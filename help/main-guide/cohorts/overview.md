# Cohorts - Overview

Get everything you need to succeed in your Adobe Premium Learning cohort. Select the linked topic name (the first words of each item) to jump to that section.

* [Cohort structure](/help/main-guide/cohorts/cohort-structure.md): Learn how the cohort works, including weekly live sessions, self-paced modules and quizzes, the Capstone Challenge, and the time to plan for.
* [Participation expectations](/help/main-guide/cohorts/participation-expectations.md): Understand what's required to complete the cohort, including twice-weekly live session attendance, self-paced modules and quizzes, and the Capstone Challenge.
* [Enrollment and setup](/help/main-guide/cohorts/enrollment-and-setup.md): Find out what to expect after you enroll, including confirmation emails, live session invites, pre-work, sandbox access, and discussion board setup.
* [Your learning journey](/help/main-guide/cohorts/learning-journey.md): See how attendance is tracked, how to stay on track with quizzes and the Capstone Challenge, and what to expect at the end of your cohort.
* [System requirements for cohort live sessions](/help/main-guide/cohorts/cohort-live-session-system-requirements.md): Check the Adobe Connect, hardware, and software requirements for your live sessions.
* [Community](/help/main-guide/cohorts/community.md): Connect with your cohort, instructor, and facilitator through the discussion board in **[!UICONTROL My Hub]**.
* [Cohort support](/help/main-guide/cohorts/support.md): Find missing calendar invites, launch live sessions from **[!UICONTROL My Learning]**, and get help with your sandbox environment and Adobe Connect.
