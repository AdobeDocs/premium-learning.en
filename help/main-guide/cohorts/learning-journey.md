# Your Learning Journey 

## How is attendance tracked for the cohort? 

Attendance is tracked based on participation in the live sessions only. If you're unable to attend a session in real time, watching the recording afterward will not count toward your attendance record. 

## Why is live attendance required? 

Live sessions provide opportunities that recordings can't fully replicate. By attending live, you'll be able to ask questions, participate in discussions, and practice alongside your peers in an interactive environment. We encourage everyone to prioritize attending each session whenever possible. 

## How do I stay on track during the cohort? 

Short quizzes are part of each week's self-study modules. These help you check your understanding of the topics and concepts that were covered. 
 
Near the end of the cohort, you'll complete a final assessment quiz of about 40 questions to wrap up your learning. 
 
You can also track your progress through the completion of the Capstone Challenge project. This group activity gives you the opportunity to apply what you've learned to a real-world scenario. 

## What happens at the end of the cohort? 

The final weeks include a Capstone Challenge to apply your learning and some certification prep time to get you ready for the Adobe certification exam. 

Cohort completion badges will be available in [My Achievements](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/premium/my-achievements).
