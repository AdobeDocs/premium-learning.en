# Community 

## Is there a dedicated support channel or forum for members of a cohort? 

Each Adobe Premium Learning cohort offers a **discussion board** where members of the cohort, as well as the instructor and facilitator, can have conversations about what you're learning, ask questions, and more. The cohort discussion board is a great way to engage with your peers outside of sessions. The discussion board becomes available **on the first day of your cohort**, in [My Hub](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/premium/home).
