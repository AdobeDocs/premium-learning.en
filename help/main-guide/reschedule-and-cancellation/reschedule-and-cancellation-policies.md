## Reschedules and Cancellations

### How do I cancel my enrollment?

To cancel your enrollment in a cohort or course:

1. Find your enrollment listed on the [My Learning](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/premium/learnings) page.
2. Click **Unenroll**.

Visit the [Terms and Conditions](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/premium/help-center/terms-and-conditions) for full information on rescheduling, cancellation and No-Show policies.

### How do I reschedule my enrollment?

To reschedule for a different date:

1. Unenroll from your cohort or course. You can find your enrollments listed on the [My Learning](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/premium/learnings) page.
2. Return to the [Premium Catalog](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/premium/premium-search) to enroll in another cohort or course date.

Visit the [Terms and Conditions](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/premium/help-center/terms-and-conditions) for full information on rescheduling, cancellation and No-Show policies.
