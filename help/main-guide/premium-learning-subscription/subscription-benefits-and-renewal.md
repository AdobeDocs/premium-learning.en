# Subscription Benefits and Renewal

Explore the benefits included in the Premium Learning Subscription and the Premium Learning + Private Training Subscription, from on-demand education to hands-on learning and expert support. Find out how your organization can renew your subscription to maintain uninterrupted access to your learning content.

## What benefits are included in the Premium Learning Subscription?

**Premium Learning Subscription provides access to:**

- Enterprise-wide access to Adobe's premier learning platform
- Unlimited access to on-demand education videos
- Access to learning environments for hands-on exercises and labs
- Personalized cohort-based learning journeys by persona and industry
- Access to all virtual public thought leadership sessions (Innovation Workshops)
- Access to post-cohort 1:1 expert mentorship for troubleshooting and specific environment questions
- Free access to Adobe certification vouchers\*

**Premium Learning + Private Training Subscription provides access to:**

- Enterprise-wide access to Adobe's premier learning platform
- Unlimited access to on-demand education videos
- Access to learning environments for hands-on exercises and labs
- Personalized cohort-based learning journeys by persona and industry
- Access to all virtual public thought leadership sessions (Innovation Workshops)
- Access to post-cohort 1:1 expert mentorship for troubleshooting and specific environment questions
- Free access to Adobe certification vouchers\*
- Access to private and personalized training
- Access to open sandboxes to practice in a fail-proof environment

>[!NOTE]
>
> Free certification vouchers are not available to All Access or On-Demand Learning Subscription users (previously with Adobe Digital Learning Services).

## My learning subscription access is ending soon. How can I keep access to my learning content?

To continue uninterrupted access to your learning subscription and content, have your organization contact your Adobe Account Manager. They can guide you through available renewal options.
