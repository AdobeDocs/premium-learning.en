# Prepare for a live instructor course

## What can I expect after I enroll in a virtual live instructor course?

After enrolling, you will receive an automated confirmation email and calendar invite(s). Your calendar invites contain a personalized meeting room link, which you'll use to attend the training. Please **accept the invite(s)** to save the dates.

Email delivery tip:

* Learning-related emails and calendar invites will come from [plsnore@adobe.com](mailto:plsnore@adobe.com) (Premium Learning Subscription no-reply email address). Add this email address to your contacts or safe sender list to ensure emails are delivered to your inbox.
* If you're missing an email, please check your spam or junk folder, and any secondary inbox tabs (such as the **[!UICONTROL Other]** tab in Outlook or similar filtered views).

## What can I expect after I enroll in an onsite live instructor course?

After enrolling, you will receive an automated confirmation email. If you receive a calendar invite, **accept the invite** to save the date.

**For in-person onsite trainings:** Watch for additional communications with details about your training location and other onsite logistics. These details may be shared closer to the training date.

Email delivery tip:

* Learning-related emails will come from [plsnore@adobe.com](mailto:plsnore@adobe.com) (Premium Learning Subscription no-reply email address). Add this email address to your contacts or safe sender list to ensure emails are delivered to your inbox.
* If you're missing an email, please check your spam or junk folder, and any secondary inbox tabs (such as the **[!UICONTROL Other]** tab in Outlook or similar filtered views).

## Does Premium Learning provide demo or training instances? How do I access my environment for class?

For customers attending a live instructor course (virtual or in-person), you will be given a sandbox environment for the class duration. Your instructor will provide the instance details and access on the first day of class.

## Recommendations for a successful training

* Be on time, and schedule other meetings at other times. Let the instructor know if you must drop for a meeting.
* Wait for breaks for reading emails, etc.
* Ask questions. Participation is more than welcome. Ideas can be shared!
* Let the instructor know if you need help with an exercise.
* Put your microphone on mute when not speaking.
* Engage in any quizzes, questions, and discussions.
